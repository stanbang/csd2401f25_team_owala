------------------------------------------------
GAME CONCEPT
------------------------------------------------
Stagebound is a physics-based puzzle platformer that allows you to rotate the environment
to affect your surroundings. Platforms may fall on you or may block off potential passages.

------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------
---MOVEMENT---
W - Jump
A - Move Left
D - Move Right

---WORLD---
Q - Rotate map by 90 degrees to the left
E - Rotate map by 90 degrees to the right

---AUDIO---
T - Play jingle SFX
L - Stop all sounds
K - Resume background music

---COLLISION BOXES---
C - Toggle collision boxes

---SCALING PLAYER---
UP Arrow Key - Scale player size up.
DOWN Arrow Key - Scale player size down.

---PHYSICS---
O - Pause the game and enter physics step mode.
I - Advance physics mode forward.
