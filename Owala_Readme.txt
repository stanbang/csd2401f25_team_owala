GAME CONCEPT

Play as Eli, trapped in a theatre filled with traps and platforms. Navigate your way out of the theatre by rotating the level and exiting through the portal.

HOW TO PLAY

Some platforms fall vertically, some horizontally, some on both axis.
Rotate the world to move platforms out of the way with physics.
Avoid alerting enemies, or drop platforms on them to kill them.

CONTROLS

W/Space - Jump
A - Move Left
D - Move Right
Q - Rotate Left 90 Degrees
E - Rotate Right 90 Degrees
F - Enter Portal

DEBUG
F1 - Show FPS
C - Show Colliders
